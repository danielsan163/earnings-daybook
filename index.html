How it works

Logging a day

On Today, type what each source earned. Press Enter to jump to the next box; Enter on the last box saves. An empty box counts as $0.

  • Under each source it shows the last amount you entered and when.
  • Use the arrows or the date box to go back and fill in or fix an earlier day. Saving a day again replaces what was there.
  • Days from the last two weeks that haven't been logged show as buttons under the form. Tap one to jump to it.
Notes

Add a short note to any day, like "new audiobook out" or "BookTok video took off". It saves with the day.

  • On Charts, a gold dot above a bar means that day (or month, in Year view) has a note.
  • Tap a bar to see its note in the pop-up.
  • Every note in the period you're looking at is listed under the chart. Tap one to open that day.

That makes it easy to see what caused a spike or a dip.

Goals

Set goals for this week, this month and this year under Goals above. Weeks run Sunday to Saturday.

  • The tiles at the bottom of Today show progress and what you're on pace for.
  • Hitting a goal sets off fireworks the first time it happens.
  • When a new week, month or year starts (Sunday, the 1st, New Year's Day), a card on Today asks for the new goal, filled in with last time's amount. Tap "Not now" to skip it.
Charts
  • Switch between Week, Month and Year, and use the arrows to look at earlier periods.
  • By source stacks each source in its own colour; Total shows one bar.
  • Year view has a dashed line for the same months last year.
  • The table underneath shows each source's share and the change from the previous period.
Forecast

Projects next month, the next 12 months, and how this month will end.

  • Pick how much history to base it on, from the last 7 days to the last year.
  • Steady average assumes recent daily earnings carry on. Follow the trend extends recent growth or decline, and can swing a lot over a year.
  • It's only as good as the days logged, so a few weeks of entries makes it much more useful.
Sources, theme and currency
  • Add, rename or reorder sources under Income sources. Tap the colour square to change a source's chart colour.
  • Archive hides a source from Today but keeps its history in the charts. Restore brings it back.
  • Pick a Theme; each one follows the phone's light or dark mode.
Keeping your data safe

Everything is saved on the device you're using. If you sign in under Sync across devices, it's also kept in your private account online, so the iPhone and iMac stay in step. Only your account can see it.

  • Sign in with the same email and password on each device. Saving a day sends it up within a few seconds; the other device picks it up when you open it.
  • If the same day is changed on two devices, the most recent save wins.
  • No signal? Keep logging. It syncs when you're back online.
  • Open the Daybook from the Home Screen icon, not a Private tab in Safari.
  • Every week or two, tap Export backup and save the file to Files or iCloud Drive. The app reminds you after 14 days.
  • On a new phone, or after clearing Safari, use Import backup to bring everything back, including notes and goals.
  • Export CSV gives a spreadsheet of every logged day.
